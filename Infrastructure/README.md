# Home Management

Welcome to **Home Management**, a Swift-based app designed to make managing your household easier and more organized. Whether you're tracking chores, managing grocery lists, or coordinating household tasks, this app has you covered—all powered by Supabase as the backend.

## What is Home Management?

Home Management helps you keep track of everyday household tasks with ease. From assigning and tracking chores to managing grocery lists and overall household activities, this app simplifies your day-to-day home organization. Plus, with secure user authentication, every member of your household can stay in sync.

## Key Features

- **Chore Tracking:** Assign, track, and complete chores efficiently.
- **Grocery Lists:** Create and manage grocery lists for your household.
- **Household Management:** Organize multiple households and their members.
- **User Authentication:** Secure login and user management powered by Supabase.
